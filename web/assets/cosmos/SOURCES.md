# Astronomy surface assets

Downloaded for local, offline rendering on 2026-11-16.

- `earth-nasa.jpg`: [NASA 3D Resources / Earth (A)](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/tree/master/Images%20and%20Textures/Earth%20(A)). Original `Earth (A).jpg`, 1440 × 720. Global illustrative Earth texture; not a live satellite feed.
- `moon-nasa.jpg`: [NASA 3D Resources / Moon](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/tree/master/Images%20and%20Textures/Moon). Original `Moon.jpg`, 1913 × 1911. Lunar photograph used as an illustrative surface material, **not** a complete, geometrically correct global lunar atlas. No claim that the mapped far side is accurate.

NASA imagery is credited to NASA / NASA 3D Resources. No NASA logo is used to imply endorsement. For the source collection's usage guidance see [NASA 3D Resources](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources).

NASA SVS's [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/) and [Blue Marble mosaic](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915/) were researched as possible higher-fidelity assets, but direct SVS downloads failed in the build environment. The two files above are the assets actually shipped; they were retrieved from NASA's official GitHub repository, not mislabeled as the SVS files.

All other planet textures, street models, stars, galaxy structures and the black-hole disk are procedural renderings. They are not photographs or exact star catalogs.
